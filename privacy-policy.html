Privacy Policy for Qibla Finder

Last updated: August 20, 2026

We built the Qibla Finder app as a completely free, offline utility. This SERVICE is provided by us at no cost and is intended for use as is.

1. Information Collection and Use

We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data or personally identifiable information.

To provide core features (Qibla direction and local prayer times), the app requests access to device permissions:

  • Location Data: The app requires access to your device’s location solely to calculate the exact angle to the Qibla relative to your position and compute local prayer times. This location data is processed locally on your device in real-time and is never saved, uploaded to servers, or shared with any third party.
  • Sensors: The app utilizes your device’s built-in magnetometer and orientation sensors to operate the Qibla compass. Sensor readings are processed purely on the device and discarded instantly.

2. Offline Functionality

Qibla Finder is designed to function entirely offline. It does not send network requests to external servers, ensuring complete user privacy and zero data leakage.

3. In-App Purchases and Advertising

Qibla Finder is 100% free and contains:

  • NO advertisements or ad tracking SDKs.
  • NO in-app purchases or subscription mechanisms.

4. Children's Privacy

Since we do not collect any personal data, our service complies with global privacy protection laws including COPPA. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
